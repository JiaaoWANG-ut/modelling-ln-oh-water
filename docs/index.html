150 ps NVT · UMA-s-1.2.1 and DPA4C-Plus

Dy versus Nd: how many hydroxides stay on the metal

With DPA4C-Plus, Dy keeps more hydroxide in the first shell than Nd under every boundary condition. Averaged with equal weight over the four boundaries and five temperatures, the bound-hydroxide count is 2.37 for Dy and 1.75 for Nd. Each number is one 150 ps trajectory.

With UMA-s-1.2.1 the two metals both stay near three hydroxides in the open box and in both droplets. In the periodic box they do not. Nd remains at three (3.00, 3.00, 3.00, 2.99, 3.00 from 300 to 500 K). Dy does not (0.37, 0.03, 1.01, 2.11, 0.15). Those five Dy runs are separate trajectories, and they do not share one outcome: the hydroxide leaves, a bare oxygen stays inside 3.0 Å, or two hydroxides remain.

Raw trajectories

All 240 trajectories are on NERSC Perlmutter, readable by every NERSC user (read-only):

cd /global/cfs/cdirs/m4517/jiaao/genesis/md/ln-150ps/runs

Layout <model>/<boundary>/<metal>/T<K>/, for example uma-s-1.2.1/box_pbc/Nd/T300/. Model is uma-s-1.2.1 or DPA4C-Plus. Boundary is box_pbc, box_nonpbc, droplet_pbc or droplet_nonpbc. Each folder holds traj.extxyz (frames every 0.1 ps with positions, momenta, forces and energy; about 90 MB), md.log, checkpoint.extxyz (last frame) and done.json. The folders also contain Er, Gd, La and Lu runs that are not shown on this page.

Simulation

  • Composition Ln3+ + 3 OH− + 128 H2O, 391 atoms, charge 0. Nd spin multiplicity 4. Dy spin multiplicity 6.
  • UMA-s-1.2.1 from uma-s-1p2p1.pt, fairchem turbo, task omol, TF32. Spin is written as the multiplicity on every energy and force call.
  • DPA4C-Plus from frozen.pt2, charge_spin = [0, multiplicity], TF32 inference. The checkpoint default [0, 1] is not used.
  • NVT Langevin, 1 fs, 150 ps, friction 1 ps−1, fixed cell. Frames every 0.1 ps. Temperatures 300, 350, 400, 450 and 500 K, one trajectory each.
  • Box length 15.782 Å. Droplet periodic cell 42.218 Å.
